# University of California: Internal Audit Plan for 2022-23

## What This Document Is

UC's real Internal Audit Plan for fiscal year 2022-23, presented to the
Regents July 20, 2022 and available at
[regents.universityofcalifornia.edu/regmeet/july22/c1attach2.pdf](https://regents.universityofcalifornia.edu/regmeet/july22/c1attach2.pdf).
Where this demo's synthetic [Audit Risk Assessment
Methodology](../../corpus/risk-assessment-methodology.md) describes
risk-based planning in the abstract, this document is what that planning
actually produced for one real, specific year at one real university
system — concrete project names, real hour allocations, and a real
staffing count, not a generic description of the process.

## Risk Assessment Process

The plan states its objectives explicitly: improving location governance/
risk management/control effectiveness, supporting management's risk
acceptance decisions, and evolving UC's Systemwide Compliance Program.
Its risk-assessment process runs through four steps: soliciting input
from the Regents, senior management, and location management; relying on
existing risk-identification processes already run by compliance/risk
functions; gathering external input (regulatory, industry); and sharing
information across the individual campus audit departments so risk
consideration stays consistent system-wide rather than each location
independently reinventing its own criteria.

## Scale and Resourcing

The plan documents 109 authorized (103 average) internal audit FTEs
system-wide for the year, with 183,735 total net available hours split
65% audits, 25% advisory services, and 10% investigations. Planned
projects total 312 (221 audits, 91 advisory projects) for 96,333 planned
hours. By functional area, planned project hours concentrate in
information management and technology (19%), financial management (15%),
and health sciences operations (13%) — a real, current picture of where
a large university system's internal audit effort actually goes, useful
context for any question about how audit resources get allocated across
competing risk areas.

## Named Systemwide Audit Projects

"Systemwide audits" are projects ECAS runs with a common scope and
approach across multiple UC locations at once, distinct from a single
campus's own local audit plan. The FY2022-23 plan names several,
including:

- **Admissions Data Analytics** — a systemwide retrospective analysis to
  identify undergraduate admissions decisions that could have been
  influenced by donations or an applicant's relationship to a member of
  the Senior Management Group — the same general risk area the real
  [UCLA Undergraduate Admissions
  Audit](audit-report-ucla-undergraduate-admissions.md) in this corpus
  addressed at the campus level three years earlier.
- **Contracting Out** — evaluating processes and controls supporting
  compliance with contracting-out requirements.
- **UC Health Affiliations** — an interim audit of progress implementing
  Regents Policy 4405 (affiliations with healthcare organizations that
  have adopted policy-based restrictions on care).
- **Executive Compensation and Chancellor's Expenses (G-45)** — verifying
  accuracy, completeness, and timely preparation of the Annual Report on
  Executive Compensation and reviewing annual presidential/chancellor
  expense reports.

## Cybersecurity Audit Priorities

ECAS's Cybersecurity Audit Team named specific FY2022-23 priorities: a
review of cybersecurity controls at UC-run combined heating and power
plants (including a vulnerability assessment and penetration test),
foundational security-control reviews at large IT service providers
across four UC locations, vulnerability assessment/penetration testing
for high-risk Office of the President systems, and a follow-up on
implementation of a prior Threat Detection and Identification audit's
recommendations. This is a real, current example of how a large
organization scopes cybersecurity-specific audit work — concrete
facilities and named review types, not the generic framework treatment in
this corpus's synthetic
[Cybersecurity Controls Testing](../../corpus/cybersecurity-controls-testing.md)
procedure.
