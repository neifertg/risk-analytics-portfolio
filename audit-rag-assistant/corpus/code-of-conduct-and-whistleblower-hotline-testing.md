# Code of Conduct and Whistleblower Hotline Testing

## Intake and Triage Completeness

Confirm every report received through the hotline, email, or direct
report to a manager is logged in the central case-management system —
reports that arrive outside the formal hotline channel are the ones most
likely to go untracked, since there's no automatic intake step forcing
them into the log. Reconcile a sample of reports known to have occurred
(from HR, legal, or manager knowledge) against the case log to test for
under-reporting, not just review the cases that are already logged.

## Investigation Timeliness and Substantiation

Sample logged cases and confirm each was assigned an investigator within
the policy's required window (commonly 5 business days) and closed
within the target cycle time for its severity category, with
documented investigation steps and a substantiation conclusion — not
just a closure date with no supporting record of what was actually done.
Escalate any case open significantly past its target cycle time without
a documented reason as a process finding in its own right.

## Consistency of Discipline Outcomes

For substantiated cases, compare the disciplinary action taken against
similar substantiated cases from prior periods and against the
organization's stated disciplinary matrix. Materially inconsistent
outcomes for comparable violations — particularly if outcomes correlate
with the offender's seniority — undermine the hotline's credibility more
than any single weak investigation would, since employees calibrate
whether to report based on whether reporting visibly matters.

## Anti-Retaliation Verification

For reporters whose identity became known (self-disclosed or
inferable), review subsequent performance ratings, compensation actions,
and role changes for the following 12 months for signs of retaliation.
This is deliberately a harder test than reviewing the investigation file
itself, since retaliation typically shows up in routine HR processes
months later, not in anything connected on paper to the original report.
