# Enterprise Risk Management and Risk Appetite

## Assurance Role, Not Ownership

Internal audit provides assurance over the ERM framework's design and
operation; it does not own the risk register, set risk appetite, or make
risk-acceptance decisions — those belong to management and the board. A
common finding is internal audit drifting into co-managing risk responses
because no one else is doing it well; document that drift as a
independence concern in itself, not just note the underlying process gap.

## Testing the Risk Register Against Reality

Compare the enterprise risk register's stated top risks against other
independent signals — the annual audit risk ranking (see
[Audit Risk Assessment Methodology](risk-assessment-methodology.md)),
recent loss events, and whistleblower hotline themes (see
[Code of Conduct and Whistleblower Hotline
Testing](code-of-conduct-and-whistleblower-hotline-testing.md)). A risk
register that hasn't materially changed in several cycles despite new
signals elsewhere is a sign the ERM process has become a paperwork
exercise rather than a live assessment.

## Risk Appetite Statement Testing

Confirm the board has approved a current risk appetite statement with
quantified tolerances for at least the organization's top risk
categories (e.g. maximum acceptable customer concentration, leverage
ratio ceiling, tolerance for control exceptions). Select instances where
a business decision approached or crossed a stated tolerance and confirm
it was escalated to the level the appetite statement requires, not
approved locally by someone below the required authority.

## Heat Map and Escalation Cadence

Review the risk heat map's likelihood/impact scoring methodology for
consistency across risk owners — a common weakness is each business unit
scoring its own risks on a different implicit scale, making the
aggregate heat map non-comparable. Confirm risks scored in the top
severity band get escalated to the audit committee on the cadence the
ERM policy requires, not only when a risk owner chooses to raise them.
