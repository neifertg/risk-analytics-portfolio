# UC Irvine PALCard (Procurement Card) Program Audit (2022)

## What This Document Is

A real, published UC Irvine Internal Audit Services report (Report No.
I2021-108, January 21, 2022), available at
[auditreports.ucop.edu](https://auditreports.ucop.edu/?action=public_ar_display&id=1a5aec18700c6d67).
PALCard is UCI's real procurement-card program: in the calendar year
tested, 1,338 cardholders made over 101,000 transactions totaling more
than $45.7 million — real, specific scale figures, not a rounded
hypothetical.

## Scope

The audit evaluated the PALCard program's administrative control
environment, compliance with University policy, and operational
effectiveness, sampling transactions from January–December 2020 across
three objectives: understanding significant processes/controls,
confirming departments manage PALCard accounts appropriately, and
confirming the central PALCard unit itself properly manages accounts per
issuing-bank and university requirements.

## Findings

Three real, specific findings, each with a management action plan and a
firm due date (March 31, 2022):

- **Post-review gap on auto-sent transactions.** Transactions with
  "super-user" approval bypass a reconciler's review and post
  automatically to the general ledger — about 7,300 transactions ($3.4
  million, 7% of volume) in the tested year. There was no procedure
  requiring any *subsequent* review of these auto-posted transactions;
  testing two sampled auto-sent transactions found no evidence a post-
  review had actually occurred. Management's action: require reconcilers
  to confirm auto-sent transactions were reconciled within three months
  and document it.
- **Outdated internal procedures.** Documentation for cardholders and
  reconcilers was adequate, but Procurement Services' own internal
  procedures for the reports it uses to monitor the program — and its
  sample-audit and cardholder-abuse escalation procedures specifically —
  were undocumented or outdated, risking inconsistent handling if the
  position responsible became vacant.
- **Undocumented purchase support.** Of 46 sampled transactions, 17 (37%)
  lacked required supporting documentation uploaded into the campus
  financial system, contradicting the university's own published
  cardholder requirements; auditors had to follow up directly with
  cardholders to obtain hardcopies.

This is a real, current example of the exact control-testing structure
this corpus's synthetic procurement-card-adjacent procedures gesture at
generically — genuine dollar figures, genuine sample sizes, and genuine,
dated management commitments.
